📖 Real Numbers — Notes

1. What are Real Numbers?

Real numbers include both rational and irrational numbers. They can be represented on the number line.

2. Rational Numbers

A rational number can be written in the form p/q, where p and q are integers and q ≠ 0.

3. Irrational Numbers

Irrational numbers cannot be expressed as p/q. Examples include √2, √3 and π.

4. Euclid's Division Lemma

For positive integers a and b, there exist unique integers q and r such that:

a = bq + r

where 0 ≤ r < b.

⭐ Important

Use the Euclidean algorithm to find the HCF of two positive integers.
