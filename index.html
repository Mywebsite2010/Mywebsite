📖 Real Numbers — Notes

1. What are Real Numbers?

Real numbers include both rational and irrational numbers. They can be represented on the number line.

2. Rational Numbers

A rational number can be written in the form p/q, where p and q are integers and q ≠ 0.

3. Irrational Numbers

Irrational numbers cannot be expressed as p/q. Examples include √2, √3 and π.

4. Euclid's Division Lemma

For positive integers a and b, there exist unique integers q and r such that:

a = bq + r

where 0 ≤ r < b.

⭐ Important

Use the Euclidean algorithm to find the HCF of two positive integers.

📖 Polynomials — Notes

1. What is a Polynomial?

A polynomial is an algebraic expression made up of variables, constants and non-negative integer powers.

Example: 2x² + 3x + 5

2. Degree of a Polynomial

The highest power of the variable in a polynomial is called its degree.

Example: 4x³ + 2x² + x + 7 has degree 3.

3. Zeroes of a Polynomial

A value of x for which the polynomial becomes zero is called a zero of the polynomial.

4. Important Types

A polynomial can be classified as a linear, quadratic or cubic polynomial depending on its degree.

⭐ Important

For a quadratic polynomial ax² + bx + c, the relationship between its zeroes and coefficients is an important concept.
